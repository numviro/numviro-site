How to calculate a pay raise

For a percentage raise, multiply your current pay by the raise percentage and add the increase to your current pay.

New pay = Current pay × (1 + Raise percentage ÷ 100)

Example

A 5% raise on a $60,000 annual salary adds $3,000 per year, bringing the new annual salary to $63,000.

Fixed amount raises

If your employer gives a fixed increase instead of a percentage, select "Fixed amount" and enter the amount directly. For hourly workers, enter the hourly increase. For salaried workers, enter the annual increase.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a 3% raise?

A 3% raise means your current pay is multiplied by 1.03. For example, $50,000 becomes $51,500.

How much is a 5% raise?

A 5% raise means your current pay is multiplied by 1.05. For example, $20 per hour becomes $21 per hour.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. The results show estimated gross pay before taxes and other deductions.

Can I use this for hourly pay?

Yes. Select "Hourly wage" and Numviro will calculate your new hourly rate and estimated annual equivalent.
