How to calculate sales commission

A basic sales commission is sales revenue multiplied by a commission rate.

Commission = Sales revenue × Commission rate

Example: 8% commission on $50,000 in sales

A flat 8% commission on $50,000 equals $4,000. Tiered plans can pay different rates on different portions of sales, so the effective rate may be higher or lower than the first tier.

Sales commission calculator FAQ

How do I calculate commission percentage?

Multiply eligible sales by the commission percentage expressed as a decimal. For example, 5% is 0.05.

What is tiered commission?

Tiered commission uses different rates at different sales levels. Higher sales can unlock higher commission rates on the portion above a target.

What is base salary plus commission?

It is a compensation structure that combines guaranteed base pay with variable commission tied to sales performance.

Does this calculator support commission-only pay?

Yes. Enter zero for base salary.

Does this calculate taxes?

No. Results are gross compensation estimates before taxes and deductions.

How much is 5% commission on $10,000?

Five percent of $10,000 is $500. Multiply $10,000 by 0.05 to calculate the commission.

How much is 10% commission on $50,000?

Ten percent of $50,000 is $5,000 in commission.

What is an effective commission rate?

The effective commission rate is total commission divided by total sales. It is especially useful for tiered plans where different portions of sales earn different rates.

Can I calculate annual sales compensation?

Yes. Enter the number of pay periods per year and the calculator annualizes both estimated commission and total compensation.
