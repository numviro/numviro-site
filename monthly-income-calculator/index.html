How to calculate monthly income

The correct conversion depends on how your pay is quoted. For hourly pay, first estimate annual income and divide it by 12.

Hourly to monthly income = Hourly wage × Hours per week × Weeks per year ÷ 12

Why not multiply weekly pay by 4?

A 52-week year averages about 4.33 weeks per month. Multiplying weekly pay by only four gives a lower monthly estimate. Using annual income ÷ 12 keeps the weekly, monthly, and yearly figures consistent.

Example: $25 an hour is how much a month?

At $25 per hour, 40 hours per week, and 52 weeks per year, annual gross income is $52,000. Dividing by 12 gives an average monthly gross income of about $4,333.33.

Monthly income calculator FAQ

What is gross monthly income?

Gross monthly income is income before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, and other deductions.

How do I convert annual salary to monthly income?

Divide annual salary by 12. A $60,000 annual salary equals $5,000 in average gross monthly income.

How do I convert weekly pay to monthly income?

Multiply weekly pay by the number of paid weeks in the year, then divide by 12. With 52 weeks, $1,000 per week equals about $4,333.33 per month.

Is biweekly pay the same as semi-monthly pay?

No. Biweekly usually means 26 pay periods per year. Semi-monthly means two pay periods per month, or 24 per year.

Does this show take-home pay?

No. This calculator estimates gross income, not after-tax or take-home pay.
