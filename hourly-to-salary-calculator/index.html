HOW IT WORKS

How hourly pay converts to annual salary

Annual salary is estimated by multiplying your hourly wage by your weekly hours and the number of weeks you work each year.

Hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year = annual salary

For example, $30 per hour × 40 hours per week × 52 weeks equals $62,400 per year.

How monthly salary is estimated

Average monthly salary is calculated by dividing the annual salary by 12.

Annual salary ÷ 12 = average monthly salary

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator estimates gross pay before taxes, deductions, and benefits.

Why does monthly pay use an average?

Months have different numbers of workdays and pay periods, so monthly pay is estimated by dividing annual pay by 12.

Can I use fewer than 52 weeks?

Yes. Change the weeks-per-year field to reflect unpaid leave, seasonal work, or other time off.
