HOW IT WORKS

How overtime pay is calculated

The calculator multiplies your regular hourly wage by the overtime multiplier to estimate your overtime hourly rate.

Hourly wage × overtime multiplier = overtime hourly rate

Then it multiplies that overtime rate by the number of overtime hours you work.

Overtime rate × overtime hours = overtime pay

Example

If you earn $25 per hour, work 5 overtime hours, and your overtime rate is 1.5×, your overtime rate is $37.50 per hour and your weekly overtime pay is $187.50.

IMPORTANT

Overtime rules can vary

This calculator is a general estimate. Overtime laws and employer policies may differ based on your location, employment status, occupation, schedule, and other factors.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does 1.5× overtime mean?

A 1.5× multiplier means the overtime hourly rate is one and a half times the regular hourly wage.

Can I calculate double time?

Yes. Enter 2.0 as the overtime multiplier.

Does this include taxes?

No. The calculator estimates gross overtime pay before taxes, deductions, and benefits.
