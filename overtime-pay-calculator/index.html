TIME AND A HALF CALCULATOR

How to calculate time and a half

Time and a half means 1.5 times your regular hourly rate. If your regular rate is $20 per hour, your time-and-a-half rate is $30 per hour.

Time-and-a-Half Rate = Regular Hourly Rate × 1.5
Overtime Earnings = Time-and-a-Half Rate × Overtime Hours

Example: if you earn $25 per hour and work 5 overtime hours at 1.5×, your overtime rate is $37.50 and those 5 overtime hours produce $187.50 in overtime earnings.

DOUBLE TIME

Double-time overtime calculator

If a 2.0× multiplier applies to your situation, select 2.0× — Double time in the calculator. Double time is simply twice the regular hourly rate.

Double-Time Rate = Regular Hourly Rate × 2

For example, $25 per hour becomes $50 per hour at a 2.0× multiplier. Federal law does not automatically require double time merely because an employee works a weekend or holiday; applicable state law, contracts, or employer policies can differ.

U.S. FEDERAL OVERTIME

When does overtime generally apply?

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), covered, nonexempt employees generally must receive overtime pay at not less than one and one-half times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek.

Some states have their own overtime rules, and exemptions or special employment arrangements can change how overtime is calculated. The legal “regular rate” may also include compensation beyond a basic hourly wage in some cases.

Important: This calculator is an educational gross-pay estimator, not legal, tax, or payroll advice. Check the rules that apply to your state, classification, employer, contract, and compensation structure.

FAQ

Overtime pay calculator FAQ

How do I calculate overtime pay?

For a basic 1.5× calculation, multiply your regular hourly rate by 1.5, then multiply the resulting overtime rate by your overtime hours.

What is time and a half for $20 an hour?

$20 × 1.5 = $30 per overtime hour.

What is time and a half for $25 an hour?

$25 × 1.5 = $37.50 per overtime hour.

What is time and a half for $30 an hour?

$30 × 1.5 = $45 per overtime hour.

What is double time for $25 an hour?

$25 × 2 = $50 per hour.

Does working Saturday or Sunday automatically mean overtime?

Not under the federal FLSA solely because of the day worked. Federal overtime generally focuses on qualifying hours over 40 in a workweek for covered, nonexempt employees. State laws, contracts, and employer policies may provide additional rules.

Is overtime pay the same as take-home pay?

No. The calculator shows estimated gross overtime earnings before taxes and payroll deductions. Take-home pay depends on withholding and other deductions.
