HOW IT WORKS

How hourly pay becomes annual salary

Your regular annual pay is calculated by multiplying your hourly wage by the number of regular hours you work each week, then multiplying that result by the number of weeks you work per year.

Hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year

For example, $25 per hour × 40 hours per week × 52 weeks equals $52,000 per year before taxes and deductions.

How overtime is calculated

Overtime pay is calculated separately using your overtime hours and overtime multiplier. A common multiplier is 1.5×, but actual overtime rules can vary by employer and location.

Hourly wage × overtime multiplier × overtime hours × weeks per year

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator show take-home pay?

No. The result is estimated gross pay before federal, state, local, payroll, benefit, or other deductions.

Why is monthly pay an average?

Months have different numbers of workdays and pay periods. Numviro calculates average monthly income by dividing estimated annual pay by 12.

Can I include overtime?

Yes. Enter your average overtime hours per week and the overtime multiplier that applies to you.

Is Numviro free?

Yes. Numviro is designed to provide simple, free calculators for work and money decisions.
