How to calculate annual income

Annual income is the total gross income you earn during a year before taxes and deductions. The conversion depends on how your pay is quoted.

Hourly annual income = Hourly wage × Hours per week × Weeks worked per year
Pay frequencyBasic annual conversion
HourlyRate × hours/week × weeks/year

BiweeklyBiweekly pay × 26
Semi-monthlyPaycheck × 24
MonthlyMonthly pay × 12

Example: $25 an hour is how much a year?

At $25 per hour, 40 hours per week, and 52 paid weeks per year, base annual income is $52,000 before taxes and deductions.

Annual income with overtime and bonuses

Regular overtime, bonuses, commissions, or other recurring compensation can materially change yearly gross income. This calculator lets you add those amounts instead of relying only on a basic hourly-to-yearly conversion.

Annual income calculator FAQ

What is annual income?

Annual income is the amount earned over a year. This calculator reports gross income before taxes and deductions.

How many work hours are in a year?

A common full-time assumption is 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, or 2,080 hours. Your actual annual hours may differ because of unpaid time off, part-time schedules, or other arrangements.

Is biweekly pay multiplied by 26?

For a standard biweekly schedule, 26 pay periods is the usual annual conversion. Calendar timing can occasionally produce 27 paychecks.

Is semi-monthly the same as biweekly?

No. Semi-monthly generally means 24 pay periods per year, while biweekly generally means 26.

Does annual income include overtime?

It can. Use the overtime fields if you regularly earn overtime and want it included in your estimate.
