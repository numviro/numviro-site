FORMULA

How to convert salary to hourly pay

Hourly rate = Annual salary ÷ (Hours per week × Paid weeks per year)

For a standard full-time schedule, 40 hours per week multiplied by 52 weeks equals 2,080 paid hours per year. Divide the annual salary by 2,080 to estimate the equivalent gross hourly rate.

Example: $75,000 ÷ 2,080 = approximately $36.06 per hour.

If your normal schedule is not 40 hours per week, use your actual weekly hours instead. A salary can translate to a different hourly equivalent when the number of annual work hours changes.

PAY PERIODS

Salary converted to weekly, biweekly, and monthly pay

The same annual salary can also be converted into common gross-pay periods:

Weekly pay = Annual salary ÷ 52
Biweekly pay = Annual salary ÷ 26
Monthly pay = Annual salary ÷ 12

These are gross equivalents. Your actual paycheck may differ because of taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, unpaid time, bonuses, commissions, payroll calendars, and other deductions or earnings.

SALARY VS HOURLY

What this chart tells you — and what it does not

The chart is useful for comparing job offers, translating an annual salary into an hourly equivalent, or quickly understanding how salary relates to common pay periods.

It does not determine your take-home pay and it does not automatically represent an appropriate freelance or contractor billing rate. Contractors may need to account for non-billable time, business expenses, benefits, and taxes separately.

FAQ

Salary to hourly FAQ

How do I calculate hourly rate from annual salary?

Divide your annual salary by your annual paid work hours. At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, divide the salary by 2,080.

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

$50,000 per year is approximately $24.04 per hour when calculated using 2,080 hours per year.

How much is $60,000 a year per hour?

$60,000 per year is approximately $28.85 per hour at 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year.

How much is $75,000 a year per hour?

$75,000 per year is approximately $36.06 per hour using the standard 2,080-hour assumption.

How much is $100,000 a year per hour?

$100,000 per year is approximately $48.08 per hour at 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year.

Does salary to hourly include taxes?

No. These conversions show gross pay before taxes, benefits, retirement contributions, insurance, and other deductions.

Why are 2,080 hours used for annual salary?

2,080 comes from multiplying 40 hours per week by 52 weeks. It is a common baseline for full-time salary comparisons.
