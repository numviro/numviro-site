FORMULA

How to convert an hourly wage to annual salary

Your actual yearly income depends on both your hourly rate and how much you work. For a standard full-time estimate:

Hourly wage × 40 hours per week × 52 weeks = estimated annual gross pay

Because 40 × 52 equals 2,080, you can also multiply an hourly wage by 2,080. For schedules that differ from 40 hours or 52 paid weeks, use the Hourly to Salary Calculator instead of relying on the standard chart.

COMMON EXAMPLES

Popular hourly wage conversions

$20 an hour is about $41,600 a year, $3,467 a month on average, and $800 a week.

$25 an hour is about $52,000 a year, $4,333 a month on average, and $1,000 a week.

$30 an hour is about $62,400 a year, $5,200 a month on average, and $1,200 a week. For a more detailed breakdown, see $30 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

$40 an hour is about $83,200 a year, $6,933 a month on average, and $1,600 a week.

$50 an hour is about $104,000 a year, $8,667 a month on average, and $2,000 a week.

WHAT CHANGES THE RESULT?

Why your actual annual pay may be different

The chart is a reference for a consistent full-time schedule. Your actual gross income can be lower if you work fewer than 40 hours, take unpaid time off, or work fewer than 52 paid weeks. It can be higher if you receive overtime, bonuses, commissions, tips, or other compensation.

If overtime applies, calculate it separately with the Overtime Pay Calculator. To combine regular pay and additional earnings, use the Gross Pay Calculator.

FAQ

Hourly wage to salary FAQ

How many work hours are in a year?

A common full-time estimate is 2,080 hours: 40 hours per week multiplied by 52 weeks. Actual paid hours can differ because of schedules and unpaid time off.

How much is $20 an hour annually?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $20 per hour equals $41,600 in annual gross pay.

How much is $25 an hour annually?

At the same standard schedule, $25 per hour equals $52,000 in annual gross pay.

How much is $30 an hour annually?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $30 per hour equals $62,400 in annual gross pay.

Does the chart include taxes?

No. The figures are gross-pay estimates before federal, state, local, payroll, benefit, retirement, or other deductions.

Does the chart include overtime?

No. The chart uses the same hourly rate for the standard schedule. Overtime eligibility and rates vary, so calculate overtime separately when it applies.
