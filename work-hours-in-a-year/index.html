THE 2,080-HOUR RULE

Why are there 2,080 work hours in a year?

The familiar 2,080-hour figure is a scheduling baseline, not a guarantee of how many hours someone actually works. It comes from a standard 40-hour week across 52 weeks.

40 hours per week × 52 weeks = 2,080 gross work hours per year

If you take vacation, holidays, sick leave, unpaid leave, or other days off, your actual hours worked will be lower. Overtime or extra shifts can make the total higher.

PTO & HOLIDAYS

How time off changes annual work hours

For a five-day, 40-hour schedule, the average scheduled workday is eight hours. Every day off that replaces a scheduled workday therefore removes about eight hours from the number of hours actually worked.

Whether paid time off should be subtracted depends on what you are trying to measure. For hours actually worked, subtract days you do not work. For some compensation or payroll comparisons, paid leave may still count as paid scheduled time.

PAY CONVERSION

Why annual work hours matter for salary calculations

Annual work hours are often used to compare hourly wages with annual salaries. A simple full-time conversion uses 2,080 hours. For example, $25 per hour multiplied by 2,080 equals $52,000 in gross annual pay.

FAQ

Work hours in a year FAQ

How many work hours are in a year?

A standard 40-hour workweek across 52 weeks equals 2,080 gross work hours per year. Actual hours worked can be lower after time off or higher with overtime.

How many hours are in a year if I work 37.5 hours a week?

37.5 hours per week multiplied by 52 weeks equals 1,950 gross scheduled hours per year.

How many hours are in a year if I work 35 hours a week?

35 hours per week multiplied by 52 weeks equals 1,820 gross scheduled hours per year.

How many work hours are in a month?

A 2,080-hour year averages about 173.3 scheduled hours per month. Individual calendar months vary because they contain different numbers of weekdays.

Does 2,080 hours include PTO and holidays?

The 2,080 figure is the gross 40-hours-by-52-weeks baseline. If you are calculating hours actually worked, subtract scheduled work hours missed for PTO, holidays, sick leave, or other time off.

Do overtime hours count toward annual work hours?

If your goal is to measure hours actually worked, yes. Add overtime and extra shifts to your adjusted annual total.
