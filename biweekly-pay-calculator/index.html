How to calculate biweekly pay

Biweekly pay means pay received every two weeks. A typical year has 26 biweekly pay periods.

Hourly biweekly pay = Hourly rate × Weekly hours × 2

If overtime applies, overtime earnings are calculated separately using the overtime multiplier and added to regular pay.

Salary biweekly pay = Annual salary ÷ Number of biweekly pay periods

Example

At $25 per hour and 40 regular hours per week, regular biweekly gross pay is $25 × 40 × 2 = $2,000.

Frequently asked questions

How many biweekly paychecks are there in a year?

Most biweekly schedules use 26 pay periods per year. Some calendar years or employer schedules may result in 27 paychecks.

Is biweekly the same as twice a month?

No. Biweekly means every two weeks, while semimonthly typically means twice per month.

Does this calculator include overtime?

Yes, in hourly mode you can enter weekly overtime hours and choose an overtime multiplier.

Does this calculate take-home pay?

No. It estimates gross pay before taxes and deductions.
