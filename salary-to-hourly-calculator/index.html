How to convert salary to hourly pay

To calculate your hourly wage from an annual salary, divide your annual salary by the total number of hours you work during the year.

Hourly wage = Annual salary ÷ (Hours per week × Weeks per year)

Example

If you earn $60,000 per year and work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks:

$60,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = $28.85 per hour

This calculator estimates gross pay before taxes, deductions, insurance, retirement contributions, or other payroll adjustments.

Why hours per week and weeks per year matter

The same annual salary can produce a different effective hourly rate when the work schedule changes. A person working 40 hours per week for 52 weeks works 2,080 hours per year, while fewer weeks or fewer weekly hours increase the calculated hourly equivalent for the same annual salary.

Salary to hourly conversion examples

Using a standard 40-hour workweek and 52 working weeks per year, $40,000 is about $19.23 per hour, $50,000 is about $24.04 per hour, $60,000 is about $28.85 per hour, $75,000 is about $36.06 per hour, and $100,000 is about $48.08 per hour.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

At 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year, a $50,000 annual salary is approximately $24.04 per hour.

How much is $60,000 a year per hour?

A $60,000 annual salary is approximately $28.85 per hour when working 2,080 hours per year.

How much is $100,000 a year per hour?

At 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year, $100,000 per year is approximately $48.08 per hour.

How do I convert annual salary to hourly pay?

Divide annual salary by total paid work hours for the year. For a standard 40-hour week and 52 weeks per year, divide the annual salary by 2,080.

Why do salary to hourly calculations use 2,080 hours?

2,080 hours comes from 40 hours per week multiplied by 52 weeks. It is a common full-time baseline, but this calculator lets you change both weekly hours and weeks per year.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. The calculator estimates gross earnings before taxes and payroll deductions.

Is salary to hourly pay based on 2,080 hours per year?

A 2,080-hour year is the common example for 40 hours per week across 52 weeks. Numviro lets you change both hours per week and weeks per year so the hourly estimate can match your schedule.
