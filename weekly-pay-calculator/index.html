How to calculate weekly pay

If you are paid hourly, weekly pay is your hourly wage multiplied by your regular weekly hours, plus any overtime earnings.

Weekly pay = Hourly wage × Regular hours + Overtime rate × Overtime hours

Example: $25 an hour is how much a week?

At $25 per hour and 40 regular hours per week, weekly gross pay is $1,000 before taxes and deductions.

Convert salary to weekly pay

To estimate weekly pay from an annual salary, divide annual salary by the number of paid weeks in the year. A common assumption is 52 weeks.

Weekly pay from salary = Annual salary ÷ Weeks paid per year

Weekly pay calculator FAQ

How do I calculate weekly pay from hourly wage?

Multiply your hourly wage by the number of regular hours worked each week, then add overtime pay if applicable.

How do I calculate weekly pay from annual salary?

Divide annual salary by the number of paid weeks in the year. Using 52 weeks, a $52,000 salary equals $1,000 per week.

How do I convert biweekly pay to weekly pay?

Divide biweekly gross pay by two.

How do I convert monthly income to weekly income?

Multiply monthly income by 12 to estimate annual income, then divide by your paid weeks per year.

Does this calculate take-home pay?

No. It estimates gross pay before taxes and deductions.

How much is $20 an hour per week?

At 40 regular hours per week, $20 per hour equals $800 in weekly gross pay before taxes and deductions.

How much is $25 an hour per week?

At 40 regular hours per week, $25 per hour equals $1,000 in weekly gross pay before taxes and deductions.

Does weekly pay include overtime?

It can. When the current pay frequency is hourly, enter your overtime hours and multiplier to include estimated overtime earnings in the weekly total.

Is weekly pay the same as gross pay?

Weekly pay on this page is a gross-pay estimate for one week. For a detailed calculation that also includes bonuses, commissions, tips, double time, and other earnings, use the Gross Pay Calculator.
