HOW IT WORKS

How to calculate weekly pay

If you are paid hourly, weekly pay starts with your hourly wage multiplied by your regular weekly hours. If you also work overtime, add your estimated overtime earnings.

Weekly pay = Hourly wage × Regular hours + Overtime rate × Overtime hours

Example: $25 an hour is how much a week?

At $25 per hour and 40 regular hours per week, weekly gross pay is $1,000 before taxes and deductions.

$25 × 40 = $1,000 per week

Convert annual salary to weekly pay

To calculate weekly pay from an annual salary, divide the annual salary by the number of paid weeks in the year. A common full-year assumption is 52 weeks.

Weekly pay from salary = Annual salary ÷ Weeks paid per year

For example, a $52,000 annual salary divided by 52 weeks equals $1,000 per week.

Weekly pay with overtime

For hourly workers, overtime can increase weekly gross earnings significantly. Enter overtime hours and your overtime multiplier above to include those earnings in the estimate.

FAQ

Weekly pay calculator FAQ

How do I calculate weekly pay from hourly wage?

Multiply your hourly wage by the number of regular hours worked each week, then add overtime pay if applicable.

How do I calculate weekly pay from annual salary?

Divide annual salary by the number of paid weeks in the year. Using 52 weeks, a $52,000 annual salary equals $1,000 per week.

How much is $20 an hour per week?

At 40 regular hours per week, $20 per hour equals $800 in weekly gross pay.

How much is $25 an hour per week?

At 40 regular hours per week, $25 per hour equals $1,000 in weekly gross pay.

How do I convert biweekly pay to weekly pay?

Divide biweekly gross pay by two. For example, $2,000 every two weeks equals $1,000 per week.

How do I convert monthly income to weekly income?

Multiply monthly income by 12 to estimate annual income, then divide by your paid weeks per year.

Does weekly pay include overtime?

It can. When the pay frequency is hourly, enter overtime hours and an overtime multiplier to include estimated overtime earnings.

Is weekly pay before or after taxes?

This calculator estimates gross weekly pay before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, benefits, and other deductions.

Is weekly pay the same as gross pay?

Weekly pay on this page is a gross-pay estimate for one week. For a detailed calculation that also includes bonuses, commissions, tips, double time, and other earnings, use the Gross Pay Calculator.
